ABOUT THE PROJECT

Groundbreaking: Sept 30, 2026 at Mokowe, Lamu Port by H.E President William Ruto and Aliko Dangote. Attended by governors from Lamu, Garissa, Isiolo, Marsabit, Turkana.

Purpose: Kenya spends $3.2B yearly on fuel imports. This refinery processes 700k BPD to supply entire East Africa via LAPSSET pipeline, road and Lamu Port exports.

Structure: 70% Dangote Group, 30% offered to Kenya Govt, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda. Funding by Afreximbank + Commercial banks.

Timeline: Phase 1 (350k BPD) in 30 months. Full 700k BPD by 2030. Training school to upskill 15k local youth.

President Ruto: "This is not just a refinery, this is East African independence. We protect worker rights, environment, and community."
